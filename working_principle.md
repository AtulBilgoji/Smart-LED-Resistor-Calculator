# Working Principle

## 1. Introduction

An LED (Light Emitting Diode) is an electronic component that produces light when current flows through it in the forward direction.

## 2. Why Is a Resistor Required?

An LED requires a limited amount of current to operate safely. Connecting an LED directly to a voltage source can cause excessive current and damage the LED.

A series resistor is used to limit the current flowing through the LED.

## 3. Circuit Operation

The circuit consists of:
- DC power supply
- Series resistor
- LED

The resistor is connected in series with the LED.

## 4. Working Process

1. The DC supply provides voltage to the circuit.
2. The resistor drops the excess voltage.
3. The LED receives the required current.
4. The LED produces light.

## 5. Project Calculation

The C program accepts the supply voltage, LED forward voltage, and LED current.

It calculates the required resistance and displays the result in Ohms.

## 6. Example

Supply Voltage = 12 V

LED Forward Voltage = 2 V

LED Current = 0.02 A

Calculated Resistance = 500 Ohms.
