# Electrical Formulas

## 1. LED Series Resistor Calculation

The required series resistance is calculated using Ohm's Law.

**Formula:**

R = (Vs - Vf) / I

Where:

- R = Required resistance (Ohms)
- Vs = Supply voltage (Volts)
- Vf = LED forward voltage (Volts)
- I = LED current (Amperes)

## 2. Current Unit Conversion

LED current is often specified in milliamps (mA).

To convert milliamps into amperes:

I (A) = I (mA) / 1000

Example:

20 mA = 20 / 1000 = 0.02 A

## 3. Example Calculation

Given:

- Supply voltage = 12 V
- LED forward voltage = 2 V
- LED current = 0.02 A

Calculation:

R = (12 - 2) / 0.02

R = 10 / 0.02

**R = 500 Ohms**

## 4. Result

The theoretical resistance required for this example is 500 Ohms.

For a practical circuit, select a suitable standard resistor value equal to or greater than the calculated resistance.
